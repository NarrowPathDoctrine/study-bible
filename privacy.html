Narrow Path Doctrine Study Bible

Privacy Policy

Our approach to the personal study information used by the Narrow Path Doctrine Study Bible Android app.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

Narrow Path Doctrine Study Bible is a Scripture study app developed by Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries. It provides Scripture reading, verse-by-verse commentary, cross-references, biblical word-study resources, personal notes, reading preferences, and a Study Verse of the Day.

The app is designed for personal study on your Android device. You do not need to create a Narrow Path Doctrine Study Bible account to use the app.

Information stored on your device

As you use the app, it may save information locally to make your selected features work. Depending on your use, this may include:

  • Personal notes associated with Scripture passages.
  • Saved Scripture, highlights, and other personal study choices, where available.
  • Reading and display preferences such as reading colors, text sizes, and appearance settings.
  • Study Verse of the Day reminder preferences and related notification settings.
  • Other app settings needed to maintain your study experience.

This information is primarily kept in the app's local storage on your device. Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not provide an account-based cloud synchronization service for these study records.

Scripture, commentary, and word studies

Scripture text, commentary, cross-reference information, and word-study resources are used to provide the app's reading and study features. The passages you read and the notes you write are processed as part of using the app on your device. The app does not require you to submit your personal notes or Scripture reading history to a developer-operated account service.

Optional daily notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app can request Android notification permission and display reminders for your daily Scripture study. Reminder preferences are managed through the app and Android's notification controls. You can disable these reminders in the app or through Android settings.

Information shared with other apps or services

Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not sell your locally stored personal study information to advertisers or data brokers. If you choose to copy, share, or export material to another app, the destination you select may process the information according to its own privacy practices.

If you choose to open a website or another external service, that destination may receive information normally transmitted by your browser or device, such as your IP address. External services are governed by their own policies. Google Play, Android, and any backup services you have enabled may also handle technical or backup data independently of Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries.

Local storage, security, and backups

The app relies on Android's security and app-storage protections for locally stored information. Protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping Android updated can help protect your study data. No method of electronic storage offers an absolute guarantee of security.

Depending on your Android backup configuration, some app data may be included in device or cloud backups operated by Google or your device provider. Those backups are controlled by the relevant platform and its settings.

Retention and deletion

Personal study information stored by the app generally remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can remove local app data using Android's app-storage settings. Any copies held in device backups may be retained or deleted according to your backup provider's controls.

Because no Study Bible user account is required, there is no separate Study Bible cloud account to close.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for Scripture reading and study. It does not require children or other users to register an account. Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated Study Bible account service.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised as the app's features or data practices change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, privacy, or the app, please use the developer contact information on the Narrow Path Doctrine Study Bible listing in Google Play.
