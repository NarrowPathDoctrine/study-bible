Support & Contact

Help with using the Study Bible app and information about how to reach the developer.

Contact the developer

For support questions, bug reports, or feedback, use the Developer contact information on the Narrow Path Doctrine Study Bible page in Google Play.

When reporting an issue, it helps to include your device model, Android version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Please avoid including private personal notes unless you specifically wish to share them.

Daily reminder notifications

Daily reminders can be controlled in the app's settings. Android's system notification settings also let you enable or disable permission for app notifications.

Personal notes and app data

Study notes and reading preferences are primarily kept on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it can remove this information. Consider whether you need any notes before clearing app data.
